How this system stays connected

HR updates only the master record. Roster, QR cards and new reports resolve the current name by Employee ID.

1. Employee ID

Never reuse or rename the ID. The QR revision is preserved when HR edits the name.

2. QR credential

A lost card is invalidated only by “Rotate QR”, not by changing the employee profile.

3. Evidence records

Attendance, checkpoint and log book entries keep audit snapshots and optional Drive photo links.
